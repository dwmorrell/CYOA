## Passages that now carry comprehension
- `bench-clue` ("A Small Detail"): now `["inference", "comprehension"]`. Gran Tull explains that fog is wet air, that it leaves mist on the mirror, and that a misty mirror makes the light dim. The reader must understand what happened and why.
